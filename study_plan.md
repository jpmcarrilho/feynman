### 🌅 Session 1: NOW (1.5 Hours) — The Quantitative Core
* **Focus**: **Week 6: Sampling & Statistical Power**
* **Analytics**: High confidence baseline ($62\%$ accuracy across 65 answered questions).
* **Objective**: Target standard error, design effect, and Intra-cluster Correlation ($\rho$) calculations to push accuracy to $\ge 80\%$.
* **Key Formulas to Master**:
  * **Intra-Cluster Correlation**: $\rho = \frac{\tau^2}{\tau^2 + \sigma^2}$ (where $\tau^2$ is between-cluster variance, $\sigma^2$ is within-cluster variance).
  * **Design Effect (Variance Inflation)**: $DE = 1 + (m-1)\rho$ (where $m$ is cluster size).
  * **Effective Sample Size**: $N_{\text{effective}} = \frac{N_{\text{total}}}{DE}$.
  * **Rule of Thumb**: When $\rho > 0$, adding more clusters ($J$) increases power far more than adding more individuals ($m$) to existing clusters.
